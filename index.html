Why transcript search changes everything

Most businesses record their calls and never listen to them again. The recordings sit in a folder — useful in theory, useless in practice. CallAmplify transcribes every call automatically and indexes the full text, so you can search your entire call history in seconds.

Type any keyword — a competitor's name, a product objection, a pricing question — and every call where that phrase was spoken surfaces instantly, with the timestamp, speaker, source, and AI score attached.

What you can search for

Competitor mentions
Find every call where a rival was named — and how the conversation went.
Price objections
Surface all price-sensitive calls and see which campaigns generate them.
Specific products
See which products are discussed most and in what context.
Outcome keywords
Search "booked", "not interested", "call back" to filter by result.
Speaker turns
Filter by who said what — agent, caller, or both.
Intent signals
Combine keyword search with AI score filters for precision targeting.

Built for teams, not just analysts

Search results include playback links so anyone on the team can jump straight to the relevant moment in the call. No scrubbing through recordings. No spreadsheet exports. Just the context you need, when you need it.

Sales managers use it for coaching. Marketing teams use it to understand what messages resonate. Support leads use it to catch recurring issues before they become complaints.
